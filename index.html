          Every BFC fighter agrees to these rules. Staff can warn, remove or ban players who break them.

          League rules

          1. Respect all fighters and staff

            Toxic behavior, harassment or hate speech leads to warnings or bans. This especially includes comments about family members.

          2. No rage quitting

            Quitting mid-fight without valid proof of a disconnect counts as a loss.

          3. No glitches or exploits

            Repeatedly using broken mechanics, stamina glitches or cheese tactics can lead to disqualification.

          4. Record important fights

            Title fights and main events must be recorded, so there is proof if a dispute happens.

          5. Stay active

            If you are inactive for 7 days or more without telling staff, you may be removed from the rankings or replaced on the card.

          6. Follow matchmaking

            Do not duck fights or refuse opponents without a valid reason.

          7. Staff decisions are final

            Admins and moderators have the final say in disputes and rulings.

          8. Have fun and keep it competitive

            BFC is a competitive league and a community. Keep it fair and enjoyable for everyone.

          Fighter changes

          You must have 3 fights on a fighter before you can change to a different fighter.

          Streaming on fight night

          How the Twitch streaming works

          twitch.tv/bfcleague

          Every fighter needs to connect their PlayStation account to Twitch before the event, so you can stream your fight straight from your PlayStation.

          1. Link your PS account to Twitch in your PlayStation settings.
          2. When your fight is coming up, join the lobby and get ready.
          3. When the previous fight ends and it is your turn, start a Twitch broadcast from your PlayStation.
          4. Once your stream is live, copy your Twitch stream link.
          5. Post the link in the #streams channel on Discord.
          The fight starts once staff can see the streams and both links are posted. If you can't stream on Twitch, YouTube works too.
          Be in the lobby and ready before your scheduled fight time. It saves everyone from waiting and keeps the event running smoothly.
